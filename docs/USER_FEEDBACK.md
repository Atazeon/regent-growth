# User Feedback Checklist

Use this checklist after testing Regent Growth with real outbound work.

## Daily AI

- Did Daily AI generate useful companies for the target industries?
- Did research briefs include enough evidence to personalize outreach?
- Were email drafts clear enough to edit quickly?
- Did the stop button behave clearly during longer runs?

## Pipeline Work

- Could you move a prospect from research to email, sequence, LinkedIn, call, meeting, and assessment without confusion?
- Were follow-up reminders easy to understand and act on?
- Were response outcomes and notes enough to keep context?

## Handoff And CRM

- Was it clear when a lead was ready for CRM handoff?
- Did owner, due date, status, and handoff notes capture the next step?
- Did CRM retry/review queues make sync failures understandable?
- Were CRM exports and summaries useful enough to share?

## Team Sync

- Was the shared-store merge behavior understandable?
- Did backups, restore preview, protection, and cleanup feel safe?
- Were backup labels and integrity messages clear?

## Friction Log

Record any issue with:

- Date
- Workflow step
- What you expected
- What happened
- How severe it was
- Whether it blocks real use
